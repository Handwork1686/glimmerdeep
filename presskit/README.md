## Pitch
The Great Lantern of Hearthmoor is going dark, and its light came from the Heart of the world. Pilot the Wick, a tiny lantern-pod, down through seven layers of glittering rock. Thread ore into chain reactions, crack geodes, find relics, upgrade a 70-node web, and relight the town.

## Facts
- Digs last 2–6 minutes, every dig banks its loot, and nothing in the deep can hurt you.
- Chains of ore, shards and explosions fill a pot that pays out multiplied when the streak ends.
- 7 layers, 5 Strata Seals, 22 ores, 24 relics, 30 boons, 37 achievements, 10 Deep Pressure tiers, and endless depths below the Heart.
