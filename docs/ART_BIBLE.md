# Glimmerdeep — Art Bible

All art in Glimmerdeep is **code-authored pixel art**. It is generated by `tools/art/*.py` from a locked palette, then packed into `public/assets/`. Rebuild everything with `npm run art`. The build also runs a **palette audit**: if any sprite contains an off-palette colour, the build fails.

## 1. Resolution & scaling
- Internal resolution is **480×270**, upscaled by an integer factor: ×4 = 1080p, ×3 = 810p on a Steam Deck window, ×8 = 4K.
- Tiles are **16×16**. The world is 30 tiles wide; columns 0 and 29 are bedrock.
- `pixelArt: true` and `roundPixels: true` are set, and every sprite position is rounded to the pixel grid.
- "Fill" scaling (non-integer) is an opt-in setting. Pixel-perfect is the default.

## 2. Palette (`tools/art/palette.py`)
There are 13 ramps of 5 steps each (65 colours). Each ramp runs from dark to light.

| Ramp | Use |
|---|---|
| void | outlines, darkness, UI frames |
| stone | rock, metal, neutral UI |
| soil / sand / moss | Rootsoil and Amberstone terrain, roots, life |
| copper | the Wick's hull and copper ore |
| light / amber | the lantern, gold, warm UI accents |
| silver | metal, silver ore, teeth |
| blue / teal | crystals, sapphire, the Grotto, the XP bar |
| red | ember, ruby, danger, damage |
| violet | the Hollow, voidglass, geode shells |

**Rules**
- Shadows shift toward violet (`void`) and highlights toward warm gold.
- Pure black and pure white are never used. The darkest colour is `#07060c` and the lightest is `#fffbea`.
- Tintable effect sprites (chips, sparks, shards, light masks) are greyscale. They are tinted at runtime with palette colours.

## 3. Light & shading
- The light source is **top-left** for every sprite and tile: the top rim is lit and the bottom rim is dark.
- In-game darkness is a render texture that the lantern **erases**. The light mask uses 9 alpha bands with Bayer-dithered edges, so falloff reads as pixel-art steps rather than a smooth gradient.
- Glowing tiles (crystals, amber, embers, lamp moss) erase a small radius of darkness and add an additive glow.
- Darkness deepens with depth: about 55% at the turf, up to 97% below 10 m. Darkness Assist caps it at 80%.

## 4. Terrain
- Each material is a **64×64 tileable texture**. The variant used for a tile is chosen from its world position (x%4, y%4), so neighbouring tiles join seamlessly.
- Each tile also has 16 **edge masks** for which sides are exposed. Exposed edges get 1–2 px of random-walk erosion, a lit top rim, a shaded bottom rim and rounded corners.
- Air tiles get an **ambient-occlusion** overlay next to rock.
- Each ore has a unique silhouette, so ores never rely on colour alone:

| Ore | Silhouette |
|---|---|
| copper | round nuggets |
| silver | thick diagonal veins |
| gold | chunky nuggets |
| sapphire / ruby | faceted diamonds |
| quartz / glimmer / voidglass | crystal spikes |
| amber | honey drops |
| fossil | spirals |
| fire opal | cabochons |
| ember | molten cracks |

## 5. Characters
- **The Wick** is 22×19. Its design: a copper dome hull, a teal porthole with the pilot's eye-glow, an angler-style lantern arm, and treads. Animations: idle (2 frames, with lantern flicker), move (4 frames, with tread scroll and lantern swing) and fly (2 frames). The drill bit is a separate 3-frame sprite, rotated to the drilling direction.
- **Relics** are 12×12 hand-placed pixel maps with a 1 px `void0` outline, one bright read colour each, so they pop out of the Codex grid.
- **Seals** are slabs of dark teal stone with glowing runes; geodes are a grey crust with violet crystal peeking through; the World's Heart is pure `light` ramp.

## 6. Decor
- Each biome has 3–4 floor pieces and 3 ceiling pieces: roots, stalactites, crystal clusters, mushrooms, vents and tendrils.
- Decor appears **only in natural caves**, never in tunnels the player digs. It disappears when its supporting tile breaks.
- Ceiling pieces sway gently.

## 7. UI
- The custom **5×7 pixel font** has full upper and lower case. It comes in two variants: plain, and outlined for readability over gameplay. Large text is the same font at integer ×2 or ×3 scale.
- Panels are 9-slice frames with a bevelled border and copper corner rivets. They come in five variants: panel, highlight, dark, and three boon-rarity cards.
- Icons are 12×12, sit on a 14×14 cell, and share an outline style.

## 8. Effects
- Effects include: debris chips tinted by material, sparks, shards with additive trails, a 7-frame explosion (dynamite only), dust puffs, hit bursts and twinkles.
- **Shards** are the game's main event, so every shard type has its own silhouette (`shard_<type>` in `tools/art/sprites_misc.py`), never colour alone. They are greyscale tintables (white body, grey and dark-grey edges), point to the right, and are rotated to their flight direction and tinted with the type's palette colour at runtime (`src/data/shards.ts`). They stay small (3–7 px) so a 28-shard burst still reads at 480×270.

  | Type | Silhouette | Tint |
  |---|---|---|
  | Glass | long thin needle | ice white `c8ecff` |
  | Crystal | elongated diamond | teal `5fe0c4` |
  | Ember | round head with a broken tail (a comet) | ember `ff9a73` |
  | Cinnabar | chunky block with a point | red `f0564a` |
  | Geode | small hexagon | violet `b862de` |
  | Vein | zigzag | amber `f7b740` |
  | Fault | jagged solid wedge | earth `a3673f` |
  | Cataclysm | four-point star | gold `ffd877` |
  | Void | crescent | deep violet `8034a6` |
  | Prism | hollow wedge | pale gold `fff0b5` |
  | Rune | square ring | blue `72aef2` |
- The Shard Bomb icon (`icon_shardbomb`) is a teal orb throwing white glints, and replaces the old flare icon.
- **Rich ground** tints the backwall warm (`ffe2a0`) and scatters gold glints across the view.
- Screen flashes are capped at 25–45% opacity and rate-limited to one per 350 ms. "Reduce flashes" turns them off.

## 9. Key art & store
`tools/art/store.py` composes every Steam capsule at pixel scale, then upscales it by an integer factor (×3, ×4 or ×8). The capsules show the village at night, a cutaway of the deep and the Wick drilling down.
