# Glimmerdeep: Steam store copy

## Short description (300 characters max)
The Great Lantern of Hearthmoor is going dark. Pilot the Wick, a tiny lantern-pod, down through seven layers of the world. Thread ore into glittering chain reactions, crack geodes, find relics, and upgrade until you tear through rock like paper. Nothing down there can hurt you.

## About this game
**Every swing pays. Every dig makes you stronger.**

Glimmerdeep is a peaceful, bite-sized **incremental mining game**. Your lantern's fuel is your only clock. Dig as deep as it lets you, then spend everything you found in the Workshop and ride the lift down again, stronger than before.

**Streaks are everything.** Drill from ore to ore and the chain keeps going. Crystals burst into shards, shards break more crystals, embers and cinnabar explode. Every coin you mine during a chain goes into the pot, and when the streak ends the pot pays out multiplied. Spark, Cascade, Avalanche... Worldbreaker.

**Dig to the Heart of the world.** Rootsoil, Amberstone, the Glimmer Grotto, Emberdeep, the Hollow, the Mantle, and at 1,500 m, the World's Heart. Each layer is sealed by a band of ancient runed stone that only a strong enough drill can break.

**Find everything.** 22 ores, hollow geode vaults that go off all at once, and 24 relics, each a little story and a permanent gift. The Codex keeps track.

**Get absurdly strong.** A 70-node upgrade web: drill heat, plows that break rock on contact, a 3x3 Excavator, chain pots that grow and grow. Refinements never run out, and the depths below the Heart never end.

**Boons every level.** Pick one of three boons as you dig. Combine *Prism Heart*, *Vein Rupture* and *Supernova* into digs that break the world wide open.

**Made for short sessions.** Digs last 2–6 minutes, nothing you find is ever lost, and nothing in the deep can hurt you.

### Features
- Hand-tuned pixel art with dynamic lantern lighting
- Original synthesized soundtrack whose layers rise with your chain, and ambience for every layer of the world
- An ending worth reaching, then endless depths and ten Deep Pressure tiers
- 37 Steam achievements
- Full controller support, Steam Deck friendly, remappable keys
- Accessibility options: screen-shake slider, reduced flashes, darkness assist, game speed 70–100%, pixel-perfect or fill scaling
- About 2.5–3 hours to the credits and many more to fill the Codex and max the web
